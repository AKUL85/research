[C1] adult_literacy_rate APPEARS INVERTED - REQUIRES A DECISION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The brief flagged this variable for zero-inflation (median 0, ~66% of
  households at zero), which is confirmed: 66.4% are zero.
  However the variable also appears to be measuring the OPPOSITE of
  literacy. Evidence from the cleaned file:
    - corr(adult_literacy_rate, mean_edu_years_adults) = -0.5048
    - corr(adult_literacy_rate, max_edu_years)         = -0.4132
    - corr(adult_literacy_rate, income_per_capita)     = -0.1185
    - Mean adult schooling by literacy-rate band:
        literacy in (-0.01, 0.0]   -> 5.42 years (n=3719)
        literacy in (0.0, 0.34]    -> 3.97 years (n=810)
        literacy in (0.34, 0.67]   -> 2.19 years (n=771)
        literacy in (0.67, 1.0]    -> 0.08 years (n=305)
    - adult_literacy_rate == n_literate_15plus / n_adults_15plus exactly,
      so the inversion originates in n_literate_15plus.
    - head_literate is a 4-category code (1,2,3,4), not a 1/2 yes-no:
        head_literate=1 -> mean head_edu_years 0.00 (n=1040)
        head_literate=2 -> mean head_edu_years 0.23 (n=1594)
        head_literate=3 -> mean head_edu_years 2.67 (n=3)
        head_literate=4 -> mean head_edu_years 6.89 (n=2968)
      Codes 1 and 2 carry ~0 years of schooling while code 4 carries ~6.9,
      so counting codes 1/2 as 'literate' would produce exactly this
      inversion.
  Households with MORE schooling currently record LOWER literacy. Read
  literally, the variable behaves like an adult ILLITERACY rate.

  ACTION TAKEN: none. The indicator is included and reverse-normalized
  exactly as the framework specifies. It has NOT been altered.

  CONSEQUENCE IF LEFT AS IS: within Adaptive Capacity this indicator
  pushes better-educated households towards HIGHER vulnerability,
  partially offsetting mean_edu_years_adults, which points the other way.

  DIAGNOSTIC (not written to any output column): recomputing the LVI with
  the literacy indicator replaced by (1 - adult_literacy_rate) gives:
    - mean LVI  as specified : 0.4954
    - mean LVI  with fix     : 0.4636
    - mean absolute change   : 0.0375
    - max  absolute change   : 0.0476
    - Pearson  corr(LVI, LVI_fixed)  : 0.9149
    - Spearman corr(LVI, LVI_fixed)  : 0.9127

  RECOMMENDED RESOLUTION (needs your approval before implementation):
    (a) Re-derive n_literate_15plus from the raw BIHS literacy codes so
        that literate = the schooling-bearing category, then rebuild the
        indicator. This is the correct fix if the cleaning miscoded it.
    (b) If re-derivation is not possible, keep the variable but rename it
        adult_illiteracy_rate and FORWARD-normalize it (higher = more
        vulnerable), which restores the intended direction.
    (c) Drop it from Adaptive Capacity, leaving 6 indicators, on the
        grounds that mean_edu_years_adults already captures human capital.

[C2] has_current_loan WAS RECODED - PLEASE CONFIRM
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  In the cleaned file has_current_loan carries the BIHS survey coding
  1 = Yes and 2 = No, not a 0/1 dummy. Verified:
    has_current_loan=1: n=3946  mean n_loans=1.68  mean outstanding=86,539
    has_current_loan=2: n=1659  mean n_loans=0.00  mean outstanding=0
  Left on the raw 1/2 scale, reverse min-max normalization would map
  'has a loan' to 1.0 = MAXIMUM vulnerability and 'no loan' to 0.0, the
  exact inverse of the framework, which treats current borrowing as a
  proxy for credit access under Adaptive Capacity.

  ACTION TAKEN: recoded to has_current_loan_binary (1 = Yes, 0 = No) and
  that binary indicator is what is reverse-normalized. This preserves the
  stated framework rather than changing it; the original column is
  untouched and is carried through to the output for inspection.

  SIMPLIFICATION TO DOCUMENT IN THE PAPER: current borrowing is used as a
  proxy for credit ACCESS / coping resources, not as debt burden. The two
  readings are opposed - a household deep in high-interest debt is not
  more resilient - so this is a genuine limitation, not merely a coding
  choice. loan_outstanding_total and loan_interest_rate_mean are present
  in the data if you later want a burden-sensitive alternative.

[C3] FLOOD INDICATORS ARE STRUCTURALLY COLLINEAR
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  flood_affected == 1 if and only if flood_depth_ft_mean > 0, for all
  5605 of 5605 households. flood_depth_ft_mean is zero for
  every unaffected household, so the two Exposure indicators share the
  same zero/non-zero structure and flooding effectively carries 2 of the
  4 Exposure slots, i.e. 50% of the dimension. The remaining two slots
  are district-level climate. Consider whether this weighting is what you
  intend before the paper is written.

[C4] CLIMATE INDICATORS ARE DISTRICT-LEVEL CONTEXT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  seasonal_wind_avg and seasonal_evapotranspiration_avg take only
  39 distinct values each - one per district - and are merged
  onto households. They are contextual, not household-specific weather.
  Half of the Exposure dimension therefore varies only between districts,
  and standard errors in any later district-level analysis should account
  for this clustering.

[C5] ZERO-MEAL HOUSEHOLDS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  15 household(s) record mealdays_total_7d = 0, with all four
  demographic meal-day components at zero. Zero meals over a 7-day recall
  is implausible as a genuine measurement and most likely reflects
  non-response or an absent household.
  These households receive meals_per_person_week = 0, which after reverse
  normalization becomes 1.0 - the MAXIMUM food-pressure vulnerability -
  and they also set the lower anchor of the min-max range for that
  indicator, compressing everyone else.
  ACTION TAKEN: none; the values are left exactly as cleaned and the
  households are retained. RECOMMENDATION: decide whether these should be
  treated as missing, which would raise the observed minimum and rescale
  the indicator for all households.

[C6] SKEW IN meals_per_person_week
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Distribution: min=0.00, p25=7.00, median=7.00, p75=7.75, max=38.00
  The upper tail sits far above the median, so min-max normalization
  compresses the bulk of households into a narrow band near the top of
  the reversed scale. This is a known property of min-max on skewed
  variables and is reported rather than corrected, since the brief
  prohibits a second capping pass.

[C7] LAND VALUE 99 IS GENUINE, NOT A SURVEY CODE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  land_cultivable_decimal == 99 occurs 17 times. Checked
  against neighbouring values to rule out a 99 = 'not applicable' code:
    95:12, 96:13, 97:6, 98:11, 99:17, 100:25, 101:10, 102:14
  The frequency at 99 is in line with its neighbours and 99 decimals is
  about one acre, so it is treated as a genuine measurement.
  Separately, 2000 households report zero cultivable land
  (35.7%), which is substantively
  meaningful - landlessness - and is retained as a true zero.

[C8] EQUAL WEIGHTING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Indicators are equally weighted within each dimension and the three
  dimensions are equally weighted in the composite, per the brief. Because
  the dimensions hold 4, 4 and 7 indicators, a single Adaptive Capacity
  indicator carries 1/21 of the composite while a single Exposure
  indicator carries 1/12. This is the standard balanced-weighted-average
  LVI convention (Hahn et al.), but it is a choice worth stating.

  'bihs_r3_cleaned (1).csv'. It matches the expected shape
