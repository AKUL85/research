## Bottom-line claim boundary

The defensible paper is a **sample-bounded methodological and empirical study** of (1) a specification-sensitive, sample-relative LVI; (2) exploratory and geometry-sensitive clustering; and (3) modest internal group-held-out discrimination of a constructed socioeconomic-tercile target using non-circular climate/agricultural features. It is not evidence of causal climate effects, validated food-insecurity prediction, early warning, national prevalence, external generalization, or deployment readiness.
